Noble Steed — luxury equestrian iPhone cases

Noble Steed is the Velvet & Valor signature series: original horse portraits, painted in-house and printed in a deep, premium glossy finish. Each case celebrates the quiet bond between horse and rider — a piece of equestrian art you carry every day, not a novelty print.

Choose from ten equestrian colourways, from soft Nude and Pink to Racing Green, Navy Blue and Burgundy. Every Noble Steed case is cut for your exact iPhone, MagSafe compatible, and finished in a premium glossy finish. Prefer your own horse? Turn any photograph into a one-of-one custom portrait case.

What is the Noble Steed collection? +

Noble Steed is a Velvet & Valor series of artist-designed equestrian iPhone cases, available in 10 colourways, cut for all 24 iPhone models.

Which iPhone models do these fit? +

Every case is cut for your exact iPhone — all models from iPhone 12 to iPhone 17. Choose your model on the product page or browse the full collection.

Are they MagSafe compatible? +

Yes — every case supports full MagSafe magnetic alignment and wireless charging.

Can I see other designs? +

Of course — explore the The Rider’s Motto series, or commission your own horse as a custom portrait case.
