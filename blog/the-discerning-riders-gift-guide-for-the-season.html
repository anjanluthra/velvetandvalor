The Discerning Rider's Gift Guide for the Season

Whether you're shopping for a competition rider or a weekend hacker, our curated selection of gifts reflects the equestrian lifestyle at its finest.

✦ The Discerning Rider's Gift Guide for the Season

Shopping for a rider is deceptively hard. They are particular, they already own three of everything practical, and a great deal of equestrian gifting is — let's be honest — a little uninspired. This guide is our antidote: thoughtful, beautiful gifts that a horse lover will actually keep.

For the rider who has everything

Start with the gift no one else can give: a custom horse portrait case. We take a photo of their horse and hand-design an artist-led portrait that becomes a one-of-one phone case. It is, by definition, impossible to buy twice — which makes it the single most memorable equestrian gift we offer.

For the everyday companion

A phone is the one object a rider carries from the tack room to the show ring to dinner. That makes a luxury equestrian iPhone case the rare gift that is both beautiful and genuinely used every single day. The Noble Steed collection pairs full-grain leather with 24k gold hardware; The Rider's Motto carries the line every dressage rider knows by heart — inside leg, outside rein.

For the sentimental

Add an engraved initial, name, or date and a case becomes a keepsake. We cover the possibilities in the art of the personalised gift — and it's worth noting that engraved memorial pieces, carrying the name of a horse who has passed, are among the most meaningful gifts we help people give.

A quick checklist for great equestrian gifting

  • Make it personal. A name, a portrait, a date — specificity is what people keep.
  • Make it daily. The best gifts are used, not displayed.
  • Mind the quality. Riders respect craft; a well-made object signals you understand them.
  • Skip the cliché. No more horseshoe keyrings.

Whatever the occasion, the principle holds: give a rider something that could only have been meant for them. Browse the full collection to start.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good luxury gift for a horse lover?

A luxury gift for a horse lover should feel personal and be used every day. An artist-designed equestrian phone case — or a custom portrait of their own horse — is far more memorable than generic stable-shop accessories.

What do you buy a rider who has everything?

A one-of-one custom horse portrait case. Because it features their specific horse, it's impossible to duplicate and impossible to buy anywhere else.
