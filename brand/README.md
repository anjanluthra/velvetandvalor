# Velvet & Valor: Brand Guidelines

This is the single reference for anything that carries the Velvet & Valor name: social posts, ads, emails, packaging and the website. The values in [`tokens.css`](./tokens.css) are the source of truth. When this file and older work disagree, this file wins.

---

## 1. Who we are

**What we sell:** artist-designed luxury equestrian iPhone cases (Noble Steed, The Rider's Motto, Cowgirl), custom horse-portrait cases, and Private Commissions (large-format abstract art of your own horse).

**Founder:** Kate Luthra, Founder & Creative Director. Her path runs from the Scottish countryside to London and then the UAE, where she came back to riding.

**The idea in one line:** *Velvet & Valor is softness and strength: poise grounded in courage.*

**More than equestrian, it is a mindset** for people who carry resilience, ambition and quiet confidence wherever they go.

**Audience:** women riders and horse owners (dressage, show jumping, leisure and "yard" life), mostly UK, US and UAE. They are proud of their horse and their sport, buy gifts for other horse people, and respond to craft, heritage and belonging rather than discounts.

## 2. Logo

### The marks
1. **Wordmark (primary):** `VELVET & VALOR` set in Playfair Display Regular, all caps, letter-spacing 0.18em, with the **ampersand in Signature Teal**. This is the default sign-off on every post.
2. **Monogram (secondary):** the interlocked **VV** in ivory (`#DED0BD`) on navy (`images/vv-logo.png`). Use it for avatars, watermarks, favicons and small spaces.

### Retire these
- The teal chevron "V" in `favicon.svg`: replace it with the VV monogram.
- The typed "V&V" in a circle used in the carousels: replace it with the monogram.

### Rules
- Clear space equals the height of the "V" on every side.
- Minimum size: wordmark 120px wide on a 1080px canvas, monogram 48px.
- The monogram goes on navy, or in navy on parchment. Never on a busy photo without a solid plate behind it.
- Don't stretch, outline, add shadows or recolour either mark beyond navy, ivory, white and teal.
- **To do:** we only have a 130×140px PNG. Ask for a vector (SVG) version of the monogram before any print work.

## 3. Colour

The palette is **navy and parchment, with one teal accent.** Roughly 60% ground colour, 30% product and photography, and under 10% teal.

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Dark ground, ink | Midnight Navy | `#071428` |
| Deep shadow | Navy Deep | `#040D1C` |
| Panels on navy | Navy Mid | `#0C1E3A` |
| **The accent** | **Signature Teal** | **`#1A9090`** |
| Teal on navy (small text) | Teal Pale | `#7FCDCD` |
| Teal on parchment (small text) | Teal Dark | `#116868` |
| Light ground | Parchment | `#F4EFE6` |
| Hairlines on light | Parchment Line | `#E5DDD0` |
| Monogram, pinstripe | Ivory | `#DED0BD` |

**Collection backdrops.** When a post features a specific case, the backdrop may pick up that case's colour: Burgundy `#5A1220`, Racing Green `#15301F`, Plum `#4E3747`, Rose `#C69FA0`. Use these for backgrounds only, never for text or buttons.

**Private Commissions** is a sub-brand with a warmer palette: navy `#0A1424`, cream `#F4ECD8`, teal `#26A3B5`, walnut `#3B2518`. Use it only for commissions content.

**Don't:**
- use gold (the website code still names the teal "gold"; it isn't gold)
- use bright primaries, gradients other than navy to navy-deep, or more than one accent in a layout

## 4. Typography

| Use | Font | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Headlines, quotes | **Playfair Display** | Regular 400–500, sentence case, tight leading (1.08) |
| Emphasis word | Playfair Display *Italic* | In teal, **one** word or phrase per headline, e.g. *When you ride, you become **someone new.*** |
| Body | **Inter** | 400, leading 1.55, no more than about 40 words per slide |
| Eyebrows, labels | Inter 600 | ALL CAPS, letter-spacing 0.20em, teal or ink-faint |
| Wordmark | Playfair Display | ALL CAPS, letter-spacing 0.18em |

The quote edition cases use a script font for the quote itself. Keep that for product art only; don't use it in layouts.

Don't use Liberation Sans, Arial or system fonts, which the old "Just Shipped" template used. Don't set long text in all caps or in italic.

**Social sizes on a 1080×1350 canvas:** display 96, H1 72, H2 52, quote 44, body 30, eyebrow 22, caption 20 (all in px).

## 5. Imagery

**The product is the hero, and the horse is the soul.**

1. **Product on parchment:** a clean cutout with a soft shadow (about 30px blur, 35% opacity, offset down and to the right).
2. **Styled flat lay:** marble, coffee-table books, riding gloves, silk scarves, dark wood, deep-red flowers. Warm natural light and a quiet, luxurious feel (see `images/cavalry-lifestyle.jpg`).
3. **At the yard:** a case in a rider's hand, with tack, stable doors, a horse's neck or a bridle in soft focus. Real light, real riders, no stock smiles.
4. **The horse:** close portraits, the eye, the mane, hands on a neck. This is the emotional material for quote and story posts.
5. **Commissions in situ:** the artwork framed and on the wall, in an editorial interior.

**Don't:**
- use cartoon horses, unicorns or novelty clip art
- use neon, heavy filters or cluttered backgrounds
- show more than four cases in one frame
- show a case cropped so the portrait or wordmark is lost

**Grading:** slightly warm, deep shadows, never crushed blacks or blown whites.

## 6. Voice and tone

**We sound like:** a composed, accomplished rider who also has great taste. We're warm without gushing, confident without shouting, and literate.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Quiet confidence | Hype, "OMG", stacked exclamation marks |
| Rider-fluent ("inside leg, outside rein", the yard, schooling) | Cutesy horse puns |
| Evocative, short lines | Long salesy paragraphs |
| About the bond, the rider, the mindset | About price first |

**Rules:**
- **British spelling:** colour, customise, jewellery.
- Use sentence case for headlines and short declarative lines, with full stops: *One of one. Artfully curated.*
- Use one emoji at most per caption, and only 🐎 🤍 ✨ or a flag. None in ads headlines.
- Name the product properly: *Noble Steed*, *The Rider's Motto*, *Custom Portrait*, *Private Commissions*.

**Signature lines (reuse freely):**
- *Carry your horse everywhere you go.*
- *Passion carried with pride.*
- *Your horse. Your story.*
- *Where artistry meets purpose.*
- *Softness and strength.*
- *One of one.*
- *Inside leg, outside rein.*

## 7. Social system

### Formats
| Format | Size | Safe area |
|---|---|---|
| Feed post / carousel | 1080×1350 (4:5) | 96px margin all round |
| Story / Reel cover | 1080×1920 (9:16) | Keep the top and bottom 250px clear |
| Square (ads) | 1080×1080 | 80px margin |

### Layout grammar
- **Light slides:** parchment ground, navy ink, teal eyebrow and emphasis.
- **Dark slides:** navy ground, white ink, pale teal accents. In a carousel, alternate light and dark slides for rhythm.
- **Every slide:** the wordmark *or* the monogram, plus a thin teal progress rule and a counter (`1/7`) on carousels.
- **Faint watermark:** a large monogram "V" at 4–6% opacity in a corner. It's optional, and use only one per slide.
- Corners are square. Pill shapes are only for promo codes.

### Content pillars (aim for a weekly mix)
1. **The Bond** (30%): rider and horse mindset, quotes, and Kate's philosophy. Carousels and quote cards.
2. **The Collection** (25%): product heroes, colourway spotlights and "which one are you?" posts.
3. **Your Horse** (20%): Custom Portrait before and after (customer photo to finished case), "Just Shipped" posts by destination, and reviews.
4. **Rider's Notebook** (15%): educational content taken from the blog, such as gaits, the terms glossary, the dressage basics and confidence in the saddle.
5. **Atelier & Press** (10%): British Vogue, behind the scenes, Private Commissions.

### Recurring series
- **Just Shipped • [Destination] [flag]:** the product on parchment with the line *Passion carried with pride.*
- **The Rider's Motto:** one riding maxim per week, as a quote card.
- **Meet the Horse:** a customer's horse alongside their custom case, with permission.
- **Colourway of the Week.**

### Captions
Start with a hook line (6–10 words), add 1–3 short lines of story, then a soft call to action ("Find yours, link in bio." or "Send us your horse."). Add 5–10 hashtags at the end: #equestrian #equestrianstyle #horsegirl #dressage #showjumping #equestrianlife #horselover #iphonecase #velvetandvalor

### Ads
- Hook in the first frame: the product and the horse are visible within 1 second.
- Lead with emotion (*Carry your horse everywhere you go.*), then proof (British Vogue, 4.9★), then the offer (FIRST10).
- Overlay text: 7 words maximum on the image, wordmark bottom-centre, CTA in Inter caps.
- Test angles: Custom Portrait ("your own horse"), Gift for a horse lover, Press and proof, Protection (drop-tested, MagSafe).
