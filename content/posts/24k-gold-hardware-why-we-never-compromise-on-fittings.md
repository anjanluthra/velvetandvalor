The first thing people notice about a Velvet & Valor case is the artwork. The second thing — usually when they pick it up — is the hardware. There is a reason the corners catch the light the way they do, and it is not an accident of styling. It is a decision we made early and have never revisited.

## The problem with plated hardware

Most "gold" fittings in the accessories world are gold *plated*: a microns-thin layer of gold flashed over a base metal like brass or zinc. It looks convincing in a product photo. In the hand, over a year of daily use, it tells a different story. Plating wears at exactly the points that get handled most — edges, corners, clasps — and once it rubs through, the dull base metal underneath is exposed for good.

For an object meant to last a lifetime and develop character with age, that is a quiet failure built in from the start.

## Why we use 24k gold

Our fittings use **24k gold** for the surfaces that matter. The benefits are not only aesthetic:

- **It doesn't tarnish.** Pure gold is chemically stable — it won't oxidise, corrode, or turn your fingertips green.
- **It wears gracefully.** Rather than rubbing through to a different material, gold simply softens, keeping a consistent warmth of tone.
- **It complements the leather.** Warm gold against vegetable-tanned leather is one of those combinations that simply looks *correct* — the same instinct that guides every choice in [our atelier](/blog/why-we-source-leather-from-the-same-florentine-tannery).

> "Hardware is the handshake of an object. If it feels cheap, nothing else you've done matters."

## Built to be ignored

The highest compliment hardware can earn is to be forgotten — to simply work, year after year, without ever demanding attention or maintenance. As we note in our [care guide](/blog/how-to-care-for-your-full-grain-leather-case), the gold on your case needs nothing more than the occasional wipe with a soft cloth.

That is the whole point. We never compromise on fittings precisely so that you never have to think about them. See it for yourself across the [Noble Steed collection](/products/noble-steed).
