Named for Those Who Ride First: The Story Behind Noble Steed

How a love of equestrian heritage and a frustration with generic accessories became the founding philosophy of Velvet & Valor's debut collection.

KL
Kate Luthra
18 February 2026
2 min read
✦ Named for Those Who Ride First: The Story Behind Noble Steed

Every brand has an origin story. Ours begins, unromantically, with a cheap phone case cracking in a yard car park — and the realisation that nothing on the market spoke to the people I'd spent my whole life around.

A gap you could ride a horse through

I grew up in the Scottish countryside, in the company of horses before I was in the company of most people. Equestrian life has its own language, its own aesthetic, its own quiet standards. And yet when I looked for everyday objects that reflected any of it, I found a choice between two extremes: mass-market accessories printed with cartoon ponies, or "luxury" goods with no real connection to riding at all.

There was nothing in between. Nothing made by someone who understood the difference between a horse you love and a horse on a greetings card.

"I didn't want to put a horse on a phone case. I wanted to put the feeling of riding on one." — Kate Luthra, Creative Director & Founder

Why "Noble Steed"

The name of our debut collection is a small act of defiance against the cute. A steed is not a pet; it is a partner you trust with your safety at speed. "Noble Steed" honours that relationship — the seriousness of it, the heritage of it — without ever taking itself too seriously.

Every design in the collection began as original artwork, not a stock illustration. It is then brought to life in the same full-grain leather and 24k gold hardware we refuse to compromise on, because the founding principle was simple: make the thing I always wanted and could never find.

Made for those who ride first

Velvet & Valor is built for the rider who checks the horse before themselves, who plans the weekend around the yard, who would recognise inside leg, outside rein without a second thought. If that's you, the collection was named for you.
