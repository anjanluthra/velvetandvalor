The Art of the Personalised Gift: Engraving at V&V

From initials to yard mottos — discover how our bespoke engraving service transforms a case into a keepsake worth passing down.

✦ The Art of the Personalised Gift: Engraving at V&V

The most memorable gifts are rarely the most expensive ones. They are the ones that could only have been meant for you. That is the entire philosophy behind personalisation at Velvet & Valor — and why a quietly engraved initial can turn a beautiful object into an heirloom.

More than a monogram

Engraving is one of the oldest forms of giving meaning to an object. A set of initials, a horse's name, a competition date, a yard motto — pressed into full-grain leather, these small marks carry an outsized weight of memory.

We see riders engrave:

  • A horse's name — especially poignant as a memorial piece for a horse who has passed
  • Initials or a monogram — understated, classic, and endlessly giftable
  • A meaningful date — a first show, a partnership, an anniversary
  • A personal motto — the words a rider lives by, in or out of the saddle

How we do it

Each engraving is applied by hand to the leather before the case is assembled, so the impression sits cleanly into the grain rather than on top of it. Because vegetable-tanned leather takes an impression so beautifully — the same quality we choose it for in our Florentine tannery — the mark deepens and softens with the patina over time, becoming more characterful, never worn-looking.

"A monogram is a promise that this object was made for one person, and no one else. That's what people are really buying." — Kate Luthra, Creative Director & Founder

The ultimate personalised horse gift

For the rider who has everything, a custom horse portrait case takes personalisation further still — a hand-designed, artist-led portrait of their own horse, made one-of-one. Pair it with an engraved name on the reverse and you have a gift that is, quite literally, impossible to buy anywhere else.

If you're shopping for a rider, our gift guide for the season is a good place to start. And whatever you choose, remember: the engraving is what they'll remember in ten years — not the price tag.
